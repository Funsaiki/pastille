Connect Spotify

to Pastille, in five steps

Pastille already shows the current song and sets Spotify's volume in Windows. Once Spotify is connected, Pastille also sets the volume of Spotify itself (even when the music plays on your phone), shuffle and repeat.

Spotify asks everyone to create their own developer “app”: it's free, it takes two minutes, and Pastille only uses its identifier.

You need a Spotify Premium account: Spotify requires it to control playback.
  1. 1

    Open the Spotify dashboard

    developer.spotify.com/dashboard

    Log in with your Spotify account. The first time, Spotify asks you to accept its terms (“Accept the terms”) and sometimes to confirm your email address: click the link in the email you received, then come back to the dashboard.

  2. 2

    Create an app

    At the top right of the dashboard, click “Create app”.

  3. 3

    Fill in the form

    App name
    Pastille
    App description
    Pastille launcher
    Website
    Leave it empty.
    Redirect URIs

    Which API/SDKs are you planning to use?
    Check “Web API”.

    At the bottom, check “I understand and agree…”, then click “Save”.

  4. 4

    Copy the Client ID

    Your app's page opens. Under “Basic Information” (otherwise, the “Settings” button), copy the “Client ID”: 32 letters and digits.

    Not the “Client secret” (behind “View client secret”): Pastille doesn't need it, and you never give it to anyone.

A problem?

  • Spotify shows “INVALID_CLIENT: Invalid redirect URI”: on the dashboard, open your app, “Settings” then “Edit”, and put the address from step 3 (Copy button) in “Redirect URIs”, “Add”, then “Save” at the bottom. Then come back here with the browser's ← arrow and click Connect again.
  • Spotify shows “INVALID_CLIENT: Invalid client”: that's not the right identifier (often the Client secret instead). Come back here with ←, paste the Client ID from step 4 and click Connect again.
  • The “Create app” button doesn't work: first confirm your email address (link in Spotify's email), then reload the dashboard.
  • This page waits 15 minutes. After that, or if you close the tab, reopen it from Pastille's Settings (the wrench next to your name), Spotify section: “Connect”.
